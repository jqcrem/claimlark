The intake technology

Better information before the first attorney conversation.

ClaimLark helps turn a prospective client’s initial account into a structured summary, with key dates, supporting documents, and missing information organized for review.

  • Structured case summaries

    Every inquiry in the same format: incident, timeline, injuries or damages, documentation.

  • Evidence and document collection

    What the person has, such as photos, reports, and records, listed so your team knows what to request.

  • Missing-information identification

    Gaps flagged up front, so the first call starts from what’s missing.

  • Attorney-defined intake criteria

    Your firm sets the case types it wants and the questions it needs answered.

  • Clear handoff to your firm

    A summary your team can review, with the person’s own account attached.

  • Less administrative work

    Fewer rounds of calls and emails to collect the basics.

Benefits and boundaries

Organized intake. Decisions stay with your attorneys.

What it’s for

  • A new source of potential cases as the discovery channel launches.
  • Less back-and-forth to collect the basics of a matter.
  • Consistent, structured summaries for attorney review.
  • Designed to support attorney-supervised case evaluation.

What it doesn’t do

  • Give legal advice or decide whether a claim has merit.
  • Estimate what a case is worth.
  • Accept clients or create an attorney–client relationship.
  • Guarantee leads, case volume, or outcomes.
  • Take a percentage of legal fees.
